A good report

What helps us fix it quickly.

An issue that can be reproduced is an issue that gets fixed. A few minutes here saves days of back and forth.

Issues are public

Everything posted on GitHub can be read by anyone. Never attach a client's project as it is: rename the project, the client and the site first, or send it to us by email instead.

  1. Search the open issuesIt may already be reported. A thumbs-up on an existing issue tells us it matters to you too.
  2. Copy your version detailsIn the program, Help > About > Copy details, and paste them into the issue.
  3. Say what you did and what you sawThe steps, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.
  4. Attach the model if you canA .rfnd file that shows the problem is the fastest route to a fix, once nothing confidential is left in it.
