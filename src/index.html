What it does, free

Everything a spread footing needs, without signing in.

The core of FoundaTrix Studio is free: model the site, analyse every footing on its own soil, and design the concrete and the anchorage, with each check citing its clause.

The Studio

Model the whole site in 3D, with drafting precision.

Draw rectangular, circular, octagonal or entirely irregular footings and their pedestals on a work plane at finished grade, by clicking or by typing, the way a drafting program works.

  • Absolute, relative and polar coordinates, object snaps and set-out grid lines
  • Move, copy, rotate, array, mirror and measure, each a single undo step
  • Model Explorer, Properties and a Command Line around a fast 3D view
  • Unlimited undo that survives a save, Imperial or metric units, light or dark theme

Analysis

Bearing pressure solved exactly, even when the base lifts off.

Under a rigid base of any outline, the program finds the pressure for every allowable-stress combination. Where part of the base lifts, it solves for the neutral axis that puts the pressure resultant exactly where the load acts, and reports how much of the base still bears.

  • Overturning and sliding on every allowable-stress combination, partial contact and net uplift reported
  • ASCE 7-22 combinations, strength and allowable stress, seismic included, each traced to its line of the standard
  • Your geotechnical report's net allowable, converted with each footing's own depth
  • ASCE 7-22 §2.3
  • §2.4
  • §12.4.2.2

Concrete design

Footings, pedestals and anchor rods to ACI 318-19.

One-way shear and flexure come from a section swept across the footing and enveloped over every strength combination; four independent mats are sized and the bars picked for you.

  • Two-way shear around every pedestal, with the transferred moment's share
  • Pedestal axial force and moment on a full biaxial strength surface, plain or reinforced
  • Cast-in anchor rods to Chapter 17: steel, breakout, pullout, side-face blowout, pryout and their interaction
  • Strap footings, the strap designed as a grade beam, by strut-and-tie where it is deep
  • ACI 318-19 §22.6
  • Table 22.5.5.1
  • §13.3
  • Chapter 17

Tables

Spreadsheet-style tables that stay in step with the model.

Every footing, pedestal, load and combination is also a row in a table beside the model. Paste combinations and reactions straight from a spreadsheet or a frame analysis, and the model follows.

  • Copy with headers, edit in your spreadsheet, paste back
  • Sort and filter any column, without reordering your data
  • Type a formula after = in any number field, and lengths in feet and inches
  • Supply your own factored combinations instead of the generated ones

Any outline, nothing approximated

Rectangular, circular, octagonal and irregular footings all take the same path through the analysis and the design.

The model checks itself
